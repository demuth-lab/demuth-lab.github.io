## Questions we ask
- How do XY males (or ZW females) survive with only one copy of sex-linked genes?
- How do heteromorphic sex chromosomes segregate faithfuly in meiosis?
- What are the mechanisms of chromatin regulation that control gene expression across whole chromosomes and how do they differ between the germline and soma?
